# Privacy Policy for Meetup

**Last updated:** May 16, 2025

## Overview

Meetup ("the app") helps you coordinate meetups with friends by tracking your estimated time of arrival to a shared destination. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

## Data We Collect

**Account information**
- Your name (provided via Sign in with Apple)
- Your phone number (entered by you when setting up your profile)

**Location data**
- Your device's GPS coordinates while a meetup is active
- Your estimated time of arrival to the meetup destination

**Device data**
- An APNs device token to deliver push notifications (meetup invites and updates)

## How We Use Your Data

- **Location and ETA** are shared only with other participants of the same meetup, and only while that meetup is active.
- **Phone number** is used so other Meetup users can invite you to meetups.
- **Push notifications** are used to alert you to meetup invites and status updates.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

## Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on [Supabase](https://supabase.com), a cloud database provider. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Supabase's privacy practices are described at [supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy).

## Data Retention

Your account data is retained while your account is active. Location data is only stored during active meetups. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us.

## Third-Party Services

- **Apple Sign in** — for authentication ([apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/))
- **Supabase** — for database and real-time data ([supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy))
- **Apple Maps / MapKit** — for destination search and routing (no data is sent to Apple beyond what MapKit requires)

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
